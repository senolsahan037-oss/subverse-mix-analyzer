Privacy Notice

This notice explains the limited data used to operate the Mix Check beta.

Data we process

  • Google account identifier, email, display name, and profile image.
  • Uploaded WAV or MP3 audio and the measurements produced from it.
  • A daily keyed hash derived from the connection IP address for quota enforcement.
  • Basic security, error, and request logs maintained by the cloud platform.

Why we process it

Account data authenticates membership. Audio is processed to provide the requested analysis. Account and network quota records prevent abuse and support the one-analysis- per-day beta limit. Operational logs protect and diagnose the service.

Retention

Working audio is deleted after analysis. Abandoned uploads are covered by a short storage lifecycle rule and may remain for up to 24 hours. The raw IP address is not stored in the quota database; its keyed daily hash cannot be reused across days. Account records remain while membership is active. Short-lived quota records and provider logs are retained only for operational and security needs.

Service providers and transfers

Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Run process data as infrastructure providers. Their processing locations and safeguards are governed by Google Cloud terms. We do not sell personal data or use uploaded audio to train a model.

Your choices and rights

You can stop using the service and sign out at any time. Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning your personal data. Submit requests through subverselab.com. We may need to verify identity.

Security and changes

We use access controls, authenticated requests, temporary object paths, and deletion routines designed to reduce exposure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Updates to this notice will carry a revised effective date.
